Engagement

How it starts

Four steps. You can stop after any of them.

  1. Consultation A call, free of charge.A short call, free of charge. You describe what you are running and what is not working. We tell you whether this is something we do.
  2. Assessment We review your environment, spend and architecture, and write up what we found — what is working, what is at risk, and what it would take to fix. You keep the document either way.
  3. Proposal and pilot A fixed scope with a fixed price, and wherever possible a pilot on one non-critical workload first. Big-bang migrations are how weekends get ruined.
  4. Run and review We operate, report monthly, and review the plan every quarter with you.
