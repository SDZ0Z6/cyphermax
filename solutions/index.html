Delivery model

Two ways we deliver — and why we tell you which is which

Some providers blur the line between their own work and a partner's product. We do not, because it matters when something breaks.

CypherMax-delivered

Our engineers, our accountability

Our engineers, our methodology, our accountability. This covers Cloud Migration, CloudOps and FinOps. When something goes wrong in this scope, it is ours to fix.

Partner-powered

Their product, our operation

The underlying product belongs to one of our cloud partners. This covers CDN, DDoS Protection and AIaaS. We handle the parts that usually go wrong: choosing the right product for your traffic and risk profile, provisioning it, configuring it properly, integrating it with what you already run, monitoring it, and raising and chasing vendor escalations on your behalf. The platform SLA is the partner's; the operational relationship is with us.

On uptime

Our commitment appliesOur uptime commitment applies to managed environments under an agreed CloudOps scope. It is a contractual commitment with a defined measurement window and exclusions, set out in the service agreement — not a measured historical figure, and not a promise about a partner's own platform availability. Partner products carry the partner's own service commitments, and we will tell you what those are for your specific configuration.

Platforms we are certified on

We work across global hyperscalers and China-origin clouds. Which platform we recommend depends on where your users are, what your compliance requirements look like, and what you are already paying for.
