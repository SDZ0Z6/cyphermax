Fit

This is for you if

  • Your site or app is fast at home and slow in the markets you are expanding into.
  • You are serving large files, video, or software updates and your egress bill is the biggest line on your invoice.
  • You have a CDN already, but nobody has reviewed the cache rules since it was switched on.
  • You are launching in Indonesia, Vietnam or the Philippines and last-mile performance is now a commercial problem rather than a technical one.
  • You need to serve users inside mainland China and your current setup cannot.

Scope

What we do

The product is our cloud partner's. The work that determines whether it helps you is ours.

Network selection

We look at where your users are, what you are serving, and what your traffic pattern looks like across the day — then recommend a specific partner network and explain why. Different networks have genuinely different footprints across Asia-Pacific; the honest answer depends on your map, not on our preference.

Provisioning and setup

Account and domain configuration, TLS certificate setup and renewal, origin configuration, and DNS changes planned so your cut-over does not drop traffic.

Cache and rule configuration

Cache keys, TTLs, purge strategy, compression, origin-shielding, and rules for the paths that must never be cached. This is where most of the available performance is won or lost.

Integration

Wiring the CDN in front of whatever you already run, including coordination with DDoS Protection if you are using both, so the two are not fighting each other.

Monitoring and tuning

Cache-hit ratio, origin load, error rates and latency by region, reviewed rather than merely collected. Cache rules get revisited as your traffic changes.

Vendor escalation

When the problem is inside the partner's network, we raise and chase the ticket. You should not have to learn a vendor's support process to get your site working.

Platforms

Networks we can deliver on

We are a certified partner across Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Huawei Cloud and BytePlus, and provision delivery services from their respective networks.

Mainland China

Serving mainland China

Delivering content from inside mainland China has requirements that do not apply elsewhere in the region — a China-region delivery product, and in most cases an ICP filing tied to a qualifying local entity. This is one of the reasons we hold partner status with Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud and Huawei Cloud.

If serving mainland China is part of your plan, start at Cloud Migration, where we set out what the deployment actually involves.
