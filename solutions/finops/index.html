Scope

What is included

Cost visibility and allocation

Tagging and account structure work so that spend can be attributed to a team, product or customer. Almost every cost conversation stalls here, and it is the least glamorous part of the job.

Waste identification

Unattached storage volumes, idle load balancers, over-provisioned databases, forgotten test environments, duplicated backups, data-transfer patterns that cost more than the compute they serve. Each finding comes with an estimated saving and a risk rating, so you decide what to act on.

Rightsizing

Matching instance and service sizing to observed usage rather than to the number someone picked during setup two years ago. Changes are proposed with the performance evidence attached.

Commitment and discount planning

Modelling reserved capacity, savings plans and committed-use discounts against your actual usage pattern before you sign anything multi-year. Where a partner programme, credit or incentive applies to your situation, we will identify it and tell you what it requires.

Architecture-level savings

The larger savings usually are not in the bill, they are in the design: storage tiering, caching in front of expensive origins, moving egress-heavy traffic behind a CDN, scheduling non-production environments to switch off overnight.

Reporting and forecasting

Monthly reporting on spend, unit cost and variance against forecast, so a cost problem is noticed in week two rather than at quarter end.

The target

What we aim for

Read that as a target, because that is what it is. The number we can actually reach in your environment depends on how it was built, how much commitment cover you already have, and how much of the spend is genuinely load-bearing. A tightly-run environment may have very little available; a neglected one may have far more.

What we will not do is quote you a figure before looking at your bill, and we would treat any provider who does with suspicion. The findings report in phase 1 gives you a specific number for your environment, with the reasoning shown.
