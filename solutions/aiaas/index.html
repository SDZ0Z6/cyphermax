Scope

What is included

Requirement and fit review

What you are actually trying to do, and whether it needs a model at all. A retrieval problem dressed up as an AI problem is common, and cheaper to solve honestly.

Model and platform selection

A recommendation across the models available through our partners, weighed on capability, regional availability, data-residency terms, and cost per unit of real usage rather than per benchmark.

Provisioning

Model endpoint or GPU capacity provisioned under a proper account structure, with quotas and limits set from day one instead of after the first incident.

Integration

Connecting the endpoint to your application: authentication, key management, retry and timeout handling, and a fallback path for when the model endpoint is unavailable.

Access control and secrets handling

Keys held in a managed secret store, scoped per environment, rotatable, and never in a repository. This sounds basic and it is the most common failure we see.

Data handling review

What data leaves your environment, where it is processed, which region it is processed in, and what the platform's terms say about retention and training use. Written down, so you can hand it to a customer who asks.

Cost guardrails

Budgets, quotas and anomaly alerting on AI spend specifically. Token and GPU costs scale with usage in a way that surprises teams used to fixed infrastructure — see FinOps.

Monitoring

Latency, error rate, throughput and spend, reported alongside the rest of your infrastructure rather than in a separate tool nobody opens.

Boundaries

What this is not

The AI market rewards vagueness. We would rather be specific.

  • We do not build these models. Qwen, Claude, Gemini, Seedance and every other model on the wall belong to the companies that made them. What we own is the infrastructure, integration, controls and cost around them.
  • We are not a data science consultancy. We do not build your model strategy, label your data, or write your evaluation methodology.
  • We do not promise a specific model's capability. Model behaviour, pricing and availability are set by the platform that operates it, and they change without reference to us.
  • This is not a way to avoid a data-protection decision. We will document what happens to your data, but the decision about whether that is acceptable is yours to make.
